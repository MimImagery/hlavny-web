This capsule represents a deep, full-circle return to the pillars that have shaped my entire life. My journey began in my youth when I graduated in Fashion Design, mastering the delicate craft of traditional lace-making, followed by a University Degree in Acting. For years, these twin passions defined my reality.

Following major spinal surgery, I spent six long weeks completely immobile. No movement. Just me, the ceiling, and a deep, forced connection to the concept of time and absolute stillness. It was during this recovery that your environmental legacy, your dedication to the earth, and the raw message of your documentary film Kiss the Ground deeply resonated with my soul. Being a dedicated vegan for over 7 years and prioritizing sustainability for even longer, it made me realize that art must have a purpose, an organic heartbeat, and a grounding connection to our roots.

Stepping recently into the historic atmosphere of Banská Štiavnica for the live film set reawakened that dormant universe. After 35 years of creative silence, I returned to the bobbins and traditional craft. Observing the costume artistry brought me straight back to my roots in tailoring, while the cinematic energy of the set provided the final catalyst to weave design, acting, and my lifestyle of sustainable luxury innovation into the organic soul of the Kiss the Ground collection, custom-crafted exclusively for you and Nikki.
