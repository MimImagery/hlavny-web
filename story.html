The Atelier Narrative

MIM IMAGERY Studio

The genesis of these collections traces back to the historic, cobblestone streets of Banská Štiavnica. Stepping into the raw, cinematic atmosphere of a live film set instantly reawakened a deeply rooted passion, returning me to a time when design and the art of acting were my entire universe.

Observing the intricate artistry of the period costumes allowed me to see fashion through an entirely new perspective. The raw emotion, movement, and dedication of the acting provided the ultimate creative impulse—a spark that breathed life into the structural elegance of The Trotuár Promenade and the sustainable soul of the Kiss the Ground capsule.

MIM IMAGERY is the manifestation of that creative reawakening, bridging traditional Slovak craft with high-end tailoring, built on the belief that art will always find a way to freeze time and celebrate the stories that shape us.
