This capsule represents a deep, full-circle return to the pillars that have shaped my entire life. My background has always been divided between two core worlds: graduating in Fashion Design in my youth, followed by a University Degree in Acting. For years, these twin passions defined my reality, shaping how I perceive raw emotion and form.

Following major spinal surgery, I spent six long weeks completely immobile. No movement. Just me and the ceiling. During that dark time, visual storytelling became my ultimate canvas. The absolute belief that even after the deepest darkness there will always be light kept me alive.

Your lifelong dedication to soil regeneration, the environment, and the powerful message of Kiss the Ground deeply resonated with my own 7-year vegan lifestyle and values. It inspired me to anchor this entire capsule in raw, organic elements, unrefined geometries, and natural textures. This collection is an artistic celebration of that raw connection between earth and human presence.

Stepping recently into the historic, cinematic landscape reawakened that dormant universe. Observing the raw textures brought me straight back to my tailoring roots, where we value the touch of the physical fiber and the truth told by shadows.
