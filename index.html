[ The Vision ]

Founded by Tina, an interior designer and actress, MimImagery is an independent European studio specializing in bespoke interior spaces, fine-art photography, and premium sustainable apparel. Every piece is crafted with slow-fashion intentionality, respecting the material, the planet, and the story behind it.

[ The Rebirth & Story ]

In late 2025, a major spinal surgery paused my world. Lying immobilized for weeks, I found my sanctuary in structural art and storytelling. This profound physical transformation didn't stop me—it broke me open. It gave me the strength to reclaim my life, return to my roots, and pick up the traditional, intricate Slovak bobbin lace craft after a 25-year pause. MimImagery is the living proof that healing is a creative process.
