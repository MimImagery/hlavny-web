The Amber Accent Pavilion

A Dialogue Between Historic Trusses and Avant-Garde Texture

This high-end residential project represents a bold creative dialogue between the traditional craftsmanship of a heavy timber structural roof and progressive avant-garde interior accents. The core narrative of the space revolves around an iconic **amber/yellow statement sofa**, which injects a vibrant sunlit energy, sharp design confidence, and a powerful artistic emotion into an otherwise strictly muted, earth-toned palette.
