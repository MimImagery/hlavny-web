This capsule represents a deep, full-circle return to the architectural pillars that have shaped my entire life. My journey began with a foundation in Fashion Design, fueled by the raw expression of materials.

Stepping into the historic atmosphere of Banská Štiavnica for the live film set reawakened that dormant universe. After decades of creative silence, I realized that true high-end design must unite the movement.

This European chapter is crafted directly for you—a material narrative calculated by millimeter, designed to transition fluidly between daylight and shadow. Every line is a structural layout; every piece is independent.
