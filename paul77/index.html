This capsule represents a deep, full-circle return to the pillars that have shaped my entire life. My background has always been divided between two core worlds: graduating in Fashion Design in my youth, followed by a University Degree in Acting. For years, these twin passions defined my reality, shaping how I perceive raw emotion and form.

Following major spinal surgery, I spent six long weeks completely immobile. No movement. Just me and the ceiling. During that dark time, visual storytelling became my ultimate canvas. The absolute belief that even after the deepest darkness there will always be light kept me alive.

While Ian's environmental legacy inspired me to ground my craft in raw nature and my 7-year vegan lifestyle, it was your striking charisma and creative intensity that breathed the necessary fire, courage, and structure into my sketches. This collection is an artistic celebration of that raw human strength and light.

Stepping recently into the historic, cinematic atmosphere of Banská Štiavnica reawakened that dormant universe. Observing the costume artistry brought me straight back to my tailoring roots, while the energy of the set provided the final catalyst. It was the spark needed to transform pain, design, and acting into the bold lines of The Trotuár Promenade, custom-crafted exclusively for your evaluation.
