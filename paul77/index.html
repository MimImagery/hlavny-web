This capsule represents a deep, full-circle return to the pillars that have shaped my entire life. My journey began in my youth when I graduated in Fashion Design, fueled by raw material expression. Soon after, architectural engineering captured my focus, transforming how I perceive spatial boundaries, structural grids, and light.

Following major spinal surgery, I spent six long weeks completely immobile. No movement. Just me, the ceiling, and a deep, forced connection to the concept of tactile reality. That physical constraint forged a precise architectural philosophy: environments should never be static decorations; they must breath, transition, and speak to human biology.

Stepping recently into the historic atmosphere of Banská Štiavnice for the live film set reawakened that dormant universe. After 35 years of creative silence, I realized that true high-end design must unite clothing and space. This is not just apparel. It is a material narrative calculated by millimeter, designed to transition between daylight and shadow.
