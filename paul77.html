The Trotuár Promenade Collection — Specially Crafted for Paul Wesley

[ Private Client Zone ]

This capsule represents a deep, full-circle return to the pillars that have shaped my entire life. My background was always divided between two core worlds: graduating in Fashion Design in my youth, followed by a University Degree in Acting. But life isn’t always as romantic as the stories we tell.

Following major spinal surgery, I spent six long weeks completely immobile. No movement. No normal life. Just me and the ceiling. During that dark time, watching your work as Stefan became my ultimate escape. Stefan’s belief that even after the deepest darkness there will always be light kept me alive, laughing and crying when my physical body couldn't. This collection is my profound, artistic thank you to the man who breathed his soul into that light.

Stepping recently into the historic, cinematic atmosphere of Banská Štiavnica reawakened that dormant universe. Observing the costume artistry brought me straight back to my roots in tailoring, while the energy of the set provided the final catalyst. It was the spark needed to weave my entire journey together—transforming pain, design, and acting into the structural lines of The Trotuár Promenade, custom-crafted exclusively for your evaluation.
